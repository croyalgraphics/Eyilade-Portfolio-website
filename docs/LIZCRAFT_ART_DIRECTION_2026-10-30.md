# LizCraft — art direction

**Visual thesis:** deep peacock blue and muted gold meet ivory paper, cotton stitches and warm, imperfect daylight. Personal, tactile and quietly expressive; the human act of making is the centre of the work.

**Central idea:** A little time. A lot of care. The original maker’s thank-you letter connects care in making with care after purchase. The existing contour device becomes a thread that links those moments.

**Content plan:** a striking branded opening; a brief honest project context; hands and garment detail; exact signature, typography and contour system; purposefully different campaign compositions; a parcel-opening sequence with real flat artwork; thank-you and five-instruction care pieces; small identity applications; digital adaptation; a quiet closing image.

**Interaction thesis:** one staged hero entrance; a scroll-linked contour drawing with an explicit replay/still option; a useful parcel sequence visitors can control. Artwork enlargement remains available. Reduced-motion, global motion-off, page visibility and offscreen states must be respected. Avoid an interchangeable food-style dish carousel or decorative 3D.

## Source and evidence

- Original LizCraft identity palette: blue `#065784`, gold `#D2A857`.
- Main typeface: Fraunces. Original guide secondary: SF Compact. The latter is documented, not redistributed; new supporting information can use the site’s licensed Rubik.
- Existing horizontal/vertical signatures, icon and contour pattern must retain their exact original paths.
- Original thank-you wording explicitly describes handmade stitches and wardrobe, supporting a crochet clothing context. Existing care and size collateral provides practical detail; new products and styled scenes are conceptual visualisations.
- Do not use downloaded moodboard/product photos as LizCraft’s real products. Do not invent founder details, launch history, collections, client approval, sales results, certifications or customer quotes.

## Lessons revisited in the supplied Behance capture library

- ANAHÍ: useful care information, tags and a natural product-use setting make a brand tangible.
- HREHEN: close material detail and restrained small touchpoints carry more feeling than repeated logo displays.
- Crochê Ternura: change the density of the supporting graphics between quiet labels and expressive wrapping.
- MARROW: let the brand voice shape campaign composition; separate a finished object from its flat artwork.
- The Common Thread Club: people and genuine activity add context, without fabricating a community or workshop history.

Original screenshot compositions and selected sampled motion frames were revisited locally. Source images remain reference material only. The new work uses LizCraft’s own identity and original compositions.

## Planned artwork, selected by usefulness

Thank-you card front/back; five-instruction care foldout; die-cut tag pair and garment label; tissue/wrap and seal; carrier and parcel sleeve; original type/contour studies; three differentiated campaign layouts; small digital story set. Photographic visualisations should show making, wearing, receiving and close printed/material detail. Combine repetitive outputs rather than pursuing a quota.

New applications must be labelled as portfolio concept development. Original supplied work is retained and can be shown alongside its new extension, with clear attribution and no suggestion that conceptual scenes document an actual production rollout.
