# Pure Hive Honey: source and imagery record

30 October 2026. Portfolio concept development by Eyilade Oridamisi.

## Identity

Exact original native logo/icon paths were recovered from the supplied Brand Identity.ai. Six source variants were cleaned to retain only painted mark geometry (17 paths per lockup; 3 per icon), with explicit viewport clipping. The public site carries the horizontal/vertical colour signatures and icon, unchanged in geometry. Original Illustrator files remain untouched.

Original guide palette: #FCEF91, #FB9E3A, #F97C00, #E6521F. Original primary wordmark: American Typewriter Bold, retained as supplied outline paths. New artwork and live text use the supplied Nunito family under SIL OFL. The Apple system font is not bundled.

## Original work versus new concepts

The original jar sample is identified as historical source artwork. Its old contact details, pack quantities and claims are not sources for new copy. No new varieties, certification, health benefits, provenance or real-customer endorsements are asserted. New label faces and sleeves are visual concepts, not regulatory-reviewed production files.

## Image production

Built-in OpenAI image generation was used. Every selected full-resolution PNG is retained locally in artwork/purehive/generated; WebP versions are in dist/assets/purehive at 640, 1280 and up to 1800 pixels, without upscaling the photographs. Earlier generated first passes are retained separately. Native SVG compositions have outlined text, exact supplied identity paths and embedded optimised photos.

Shared constraints: photographic realism, no rustic wood/farmhouse styling, no CGI/plastic skin, no collage, no added claims or pack sizes, no watermark. Exact supplied logo references guided the branded scenes. A targeted edit changed the initial dark logo separator to the correct pale negative-space gap while preserving the surrounding scenes. People are fictional models; representation descriptors guide casting and do not establish nationality.

## Reference learning

19 supplied screenshots from JAMMY, KAMAKURA, Arôme and Tigris were visually examined in the bounded source audit. Applied lessons: a consistent label architecture, preparation close-ups, a connection between pack and everyday use, and controlled materials. No reference-brand imagery is included in the public artwork.
