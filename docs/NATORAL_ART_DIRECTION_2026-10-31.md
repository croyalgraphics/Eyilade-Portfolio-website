# Natoral: nature's care, made tangible

## Visual thesis

An expressive green identity meets precise, tactile packaging and unforced human moments in bright contemporary spaces: nature-first, colourful and considered, without apothecary clichés or artificial floating products.

## Content plan

An unmistakable original-logo/product opening; concise real-brand context; a range architecture with four related worlds; exact original identity and pattern; named packaging face studies; body and hair rituals; a clearly separated baby-food/supplement presentation; active nutrition; material details and campaign artwork; original source comparison; a confident brand close.

## Interaction thesis

1. Staged logo, short headline and full-width product image entrance.
2. A visitor-controlled range selector showing each range's physical pack, proposed names and flat artwork together, with stable layout and keyboard-operable buttons.
3. A restrained, scroll-linked change of scale in the original motif, plus clear artwork zoom affordances. Reduced motion uses the settled composition.

## Confirmed and proposed content

The user confirms Natoral is a real nature-first brand producing body care, haircare, baby supplements, baby foods and bodybuilding products. Source identity remains the foundation. ## Study, 31 October 2026

Revisited nine supplied captures across SAVONRY, Dr.Parrot and the a/2 body-care project, plus selected close views. SAVONRY makes a coordinated range tangible through print, pack and hands; Dr.Parrot shows a consistent hierarchy across varied formats; a/2 demonstrates material control and human scale. Natoral's geometry, names, layout and green system are its own. Reference artworks are not included in deliverables.

Reviewed Aveda's [Botanical Repair range](https://www.aveda.com/botanical-repair-hair-damage-treatment), Nestlé's [CERELAC range](https://www.parenteam.com.ph/cerelac/our-products-cerelac) and [Optimum Nutrition's product navigation](https://www.optimumnutrition.com/en-us). The useful lesson is clear family/descriptor/variant hierarchy and differentiation by product use. Their ingredients, claims, labels and marks are not copied.

Consulted [NAFDAC's MSME guidance](https://nafdac.gov.ng/our-services/msme/) to distinguish concept faces from complete operating labels. Product-specific substantiation and statutory information cannot be invented by design. This study is not a legal compliance assessment.

## Imagery

Fictional African, white and Asian models, varied ages and genders where appropriate; candid gestures and realistic skin/hair. Stone, glass, modern architecture and natural light. No rustic wood, distressed plaster, laboratory clichés or fake founder/customer claims. All new imagery is identified as AI-assisted portfolio visualisation.
