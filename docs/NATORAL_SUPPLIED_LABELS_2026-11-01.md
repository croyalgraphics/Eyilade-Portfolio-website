## Method

Keep all eight originals byte-identical and record their SHA-256 hashes. Embed their RGBA cutouts directly into native SVG compositions. Do not ask the image model to redraw, reinterpret or retype these labels. Generate only surrounding photographic scenes with clear product-placement space. Pack pixels are scaled uniformly, never stretched; alpha silhouettes remain intact. Native SVGs embed the original PNG bytes. Responsive WebP exports are derived from the composed artwork.

The supplied designs contain ingredient, nutrition, age and benefit copy. Preserve that artwork as supplied; do not expand it into new factual promises in website prose. The case study presents packaging artwork, not independent verification of label statements or production approval. Model scenes are fictional AI-assisted visualisations.

## Composition

Body: pink and burgundy packs in a bright modern beauty scene. Hair: cobalt and teal against a fresh tiled interior. Baby: warm cereal and lavender supplement packs with separate space and clear purpose. Fitness: black tubs in a contemporary gym. The source logo presentation remains as historical identity work; new typography, pattern and dispatch studies must not masquerade as replacement product labels.

Replace hero, cover, category photos, category artwork boards, detail, lifestyle pack placements, campaign posters and digital stories. Keep image zoom, range switching, reduced-motion behaviour and responsive navigation working.
