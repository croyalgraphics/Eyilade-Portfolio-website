# Beans Central — art direction and production

Visual thesis: appetising West African bean dishes, warm direct light, tactile food-service paper and a playful but disciplined red/ochre/brown identity.

Organising idea: **There’s more to beans.** Six original dishes reveal range, with a recognisable plate/bean graphic vocabulary linking food communication and physical applications. Preserve the original logo and icon exactly; retain #E0012E / #DB8A1F / #2D120E and Quicksand body / Fredoka display.

Content plan: a finished meal/brand image; concise existing-work/new-extension context; an exact identity and type system; original supporting graphics; six dish campaigns; readable menu; flat packaging artwork plus realistic applications; digital adaptations; a short original graphic sequence; a final brand-world scene.

Interaction thesis: restrained entrance of title/mark; a compact animated identity-to-dish sequence; large still artwork with explicit enlargement controls. Follow the website’s motion preference, reduced-motion and offscreen pause rules. No heavy runtime added for presentation effects.

Source discovery: 11-page PDF-compatible Illustrator source, six embedded food photographs/campaigns. Current website shows only four. Recover original vectors, images, and local source fonts first. New application artwork is portfolio concept development, distinct from original campaign artwork; no fabricated prices, client results or production claims.

Scope: Beans Central case study, its own thumbnail/preview, and its original art deliverables. Other case studies and Works landing-page composition stay outside this production pass. Previously requested project removals will be handled as a separate content edit so the Beans Central work can be assessed independently.
