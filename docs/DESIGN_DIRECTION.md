# Eyilade Oridamisi — portfolio direction

Visual thesis: a colourful editorial folio with cobalt typography, warm paper, a tactile sense of depth, and generous space for the designer's actual work.

Content plan: a personal opening; six selected identities and visual projects; a concise introduction and experience; a small collection of motion, print and 3D work; a direct invitation for clients and recruiters to get in touch. Every project has its own address and an honest account of the supplied work.

Interaction thesis: a short typographic entrance; a lightweight interactive cube displaying original identity artwork; gentle project reveals, image transitions and a playful interactive sketch that communicates curiosity. Reduced motion and touch have considered alternatives.

## Content boundaries

Private software concepts are excluded from public pages, metadata, downloads, assets and source packaging. Their general influence is expressed through curiosity, systems thinking and AI-assisted prototyping. No product screenshots, functionality, domains, source code or performance claims are used. The user subsequently explicitly requested all page 5 logo artworks for a public animated logofolio; these marks and their names are included as visual design only, with no connection to private product plans.

The supplied portfolio includes a private concept in its logo index. The public download is a curated selection that omits the index and logo sheets. The public CV is a separate concise edition based on verified professional history and excludes internal product initiatives. Originals remain untouched.

No invented testimonials, awards, metrics, launch histories or client results. 3D studies are labelled as studies. Site claims describe visible deliverables rather than unverified business outcomes.
