# Portfolio imagery direction

Approved by Eyilade on 30 October 2026.

Use colourful, photorealistic human scenes and believable physical mockups across future project studies. Cast fictional African, white and Asian models with varied genders and ages where appropriate to the brand. Include natural expressions, everyday gestures, tactile materials and convincing modern settings. Avoid repetitive stock poses and artificial-looking faces.

For the LizCraft expansion, include white adult male and female models and children in wholesome fashion/family scenes, alongside the existing imagery. Preserve the blue-and-gold identity and the supplied maker voice.

People in newly generated imagery are fictional models, not represented as actual founders, staff, customers or endorsers. Keep AI-assisted portfolio concept imagery distinguishable from supplied original work. Do not invent product claims, certification or commercial outcomes. For honey scenes, children are clearly school-aged.

Environment direction: contemporary homes and modern scenes, clean stone surfaces, modern furniture, bright interiors and natural daylight. Avoid rustic, weathered-wood, farmhouse or distressed-plaster styling. This applies to both LizCraft and Pure Hive and future project imagery.

Product direction for LizCraft: the identity palette is not a limit on product colour. Develop colourful wardrobe, bag, accessory and home-textile concepts (dresses, cushions, throws, small crochet charms), with rich colour and varied stitch scales. User-supplied Crochet Ideas images are inspiration, not proof of LizCraft inventory and not assets to copy into the public study.
