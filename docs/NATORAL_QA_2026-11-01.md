## Scope

Expanded the existing identity into four proposed product families covering the user's confirmed body-care, haircare, baby-food, baby-supplement and bodybuilding categories. Developed seven product names across eight pack concepts, 14 native SVG/PNG brand compositions and nine selected AI-assisted photographic concepts. Original source work is preserved.

Proposed names: Dewskin, Softmelt, Root Ritual, Curl Veil, First Spoon, Little Drops and Form Daily (Vanilla/Cocoa concept variants). New names, sizes and packaging faces are clearly identified as portfolio development rather than an existing commercial catalogue or sale-approved packaging. No formulations, age suitability, dosage, nutrition values, certifications or performance results are invented.
