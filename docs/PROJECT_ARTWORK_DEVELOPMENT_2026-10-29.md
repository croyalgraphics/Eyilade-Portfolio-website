## Recommended first project: Beans Central

### Visual thesis

Warm, confident everyday food communication: make the actual bean dishes appetising and distinctive, anchored by the existing friendly bean-and-fork signature, red/ochre/brown palette and rounded type.

The current guide identifies Quicksand and Fredoka. Match approved source font roles and exact colour values before production. Keep the supplied icon and wordmark intact. The brand is a food identity; the reference material does not justify turning it into a coffee brand.

### The system to develop

- **Signature:** approved primary logo, icon and clear-space treatment. Show real supplied variants rather than generating alternate logos.
- **Graphic language:** derive a small original family from bean, bowl, plate and fork geometry. Establish when a shape can become an image crop, a divider or a quiet supporting pattern. Keep it independent of protected logo clear space.
- **Typography:** a consistent hierarchy for dish name, supporting line and useful information. Display type can move between curved and straight compositions; it should not require every layout to be the same circular dish poster.
- **Photography/art direction:** maintain the correct appearance of the actual dishes. Use coherent light, food scale, surfaces and styling. Combine a strong overhead meal image with tactile close-ups and a takeaway/use scene.
- **Voice:** short, warm and food-specific. Draft original campaign language when creating the artwork; do not transplant reference slogans.

### References and the specific lesson to transfer

- **Midday:** stable geometric rules across multiple food-service formats. Transfer the system discipline, not the blue/yellow palette, lettering or zigzag.
- **MUNCHY:** recognition across storefront, food, people and takeaway. Give Beans Central its own contexts and image treatment.
- **Bowl & Chill:** a logo can sit within a small supporting illustration world. Derive that world from Beans Central’s own character.
- **Pasty:** product/package silhouettes make a visual system tangible. Choose packaging that suits the evidenced dishes rather than copying pasta tubes.
- **BRUMO:** small touchpoints can be as expressive as a hero mockup. Develop a useful seal, menu and liner before unrelated merchandise.
- **Crochet Stories:** differentiate individual items within a consistent campaign. A dish series should allow real variety of composition.

### Case-study sequence

1. Strong finished meal/pack scene.
2. A short, truthful account of the original identity and the new portfolio development.
3. Signature, type, colour and supporting graphic rules.
4. Menu and dish campaign artwork, with close readable details.
5. Flat packaging artwork followed by container/carrier applications.
6. One physical use scene, one digital adaptation group and one short motion moment.
7. A concise final composition showing the family together.

The presentation should alternate large single images, paired details and occasional system overviews. A long gallery of interchangeable device/mockup images would weaken the work. Caption what a piece demonstrates without repeating the brand description.

### Content and production decisions

Use approved information from the supplied work. Confirm current menu/prices, contact details, pack sizes and any claims before placing them on realistic new applications. Do not manufacture nutritional data, testimonials, measured results, client approval or a live ordering destination. New portfolio applications should be labelled as new concept development, so the boundary between the existing work and the expansion remains clear.

Build exact brand graphics and layout as editable vector/type artwork. Use original photography or carefully art-directed visual generation for scenes and props when appropriate, then place the real logo and typography accurately. Keep editable masters, flat exports, contextual visuals and responsive website exports as distinct deliverables. Mockups are a way to present designed artwork; each pack/menu/poster must also exist as its own intentional composition.

## Other individual projects

### Feasts & Flavours

**Direction:** a generous shared-table identity rooted in its existing red ornamental mark and lively wordmark. Retain the supplied Funky Bird/Fredoka roles subject to source verification. Differentiate it from Beans Central through a more celebratory serving context, restrained use of the ornamental centrepiece and a distinctive editorial rhythm.

**Develop:** a menu, food-service packaging/band, table or takeaway collateral, a small original serving/ingredient illustration family, three related campaign posters, a story adaptation and a full flat-artwork view. Use the supplied food flyers and actual represented dishes. If catering is not confirmed, describe proposed table/service applications as concepts rather than existing business services.

**References:** BRUMO for sharing and small touchpoints; Arôme for food illustration and practical collateral; CluckRiot for coordinated food communication. Avoid reproducing CluckRiot’s heat/flame personality.

### LizCraft

**Direction:** tactile, carefully made blue/gold identity. The existing sculpted monogram and repeated contours should provide the organising language.

**Develop:** a clear lockup/pattern system, quiet and dense pattern treatments, tissue or parcel wrap, a seal, thank-you card, hangtag/care card where relevant, bag detail and a small digital campaign family. Include close paper/print views and a scene demonstrating how the pieces belong together. Confirm the actual retail/craft product range before adding apparel, crochet objects or collection names.

**References:** ANAHÍ for useful product/care communication; HREHEN for restrained craft presentation; Crochê Ternura for tactile small surfaces; MARROW for the distinction between brand voice and application. No claim of workshops, a craft community or events without supporting information.

### Natoral

**Develop now:** accurate identity board, pattern scale/density studies, type hierarchy, icon-to-graphic transformation and a small set of original communication layouts. ### Pure Hive Honey

**Direction:** a warm product-led world built from the honeycomb/flow gesture, amber and the supplied wordmark.

**Develop:** accurate front/back jar artwork, lid seal, packaging band or outer carton concept, flat-artwork view, a restrained spoon/pour composition, one serving/use scene and a short range/mark animation. Recover/verify the actual logo and label first. A single excellent jar scene plus clearly designed artwork is stronger than a fabricated assortment.

**References:** JAMMY for consistent label families; KAMAKURA for ingredient/preparation detail; Tigris for warm material control; Arôme for connecting packaging with food use. Do not invent honey varieties, provenance, certifications or health claims.

### Doc’s Apothecary

**Develop:** product-line architecture based on the actual labels; readable flat label plates; front/back container views; a range group; a few close material/typography details; product-specific campaign compositions and a useful print/layout explanation.
