# LizCraft human expansion and Pure Hive Honey

## Visual thesis

Modern life, colour and real human presence: tactile crochet and translucent honey photographed in bright contemporary interiors, supported by each brand's original identity.

## LizCraft

Retain the exact blue/gold signature, contour and maker wording. Broaden product concepts to colourful dresses, shirts, bags, charms, cushions and throws. Show white adult men and women and school-aged children, African fashion models and Asian making imagery, with natural gestures. Modern apartments, white stone, glass and chrome replace rustic wood. Collection concepts are not claims about existing stock.

Content: contemporary making hero; wearable colour and accessories; family/home moment; exact identity and contour; campaigns; parcel concepts; original thank-you and care system; original work.

## Pure Hive Honey

Use exact native wordmarks and icon from Brand Identity.ai, palette #FCEF91 / #FB9E3A / #F97C00 / #E6521F. Retain American Typewriter's supplied outlined wordmark; use licensed Nunito for all new type. Do not distribute the Apple font.

Content progression: strong photographic jar/brand hero; concise source-to-extension context; identity and palette; native label/lid/sleeve artwork and physical packaging concepts; a shared modern breakfast with African, white and Asian adults; individual everyday food moments; distinct campaign posters and small print; original packaging provenance.

Photography: contemporary kitchen/dining spaces, pale stone, clean glass, brushed steel, expressive orange and lemon. Realistic skin and hands, material texture, controlled reflections, natural daylight. No rustic/farmhouse environments. Fictional models, no client or customer endorsements. New packaging artwork does not introduce varieties, weight claims, certification, efficacy, producer facts or old contact details.

## Interaction thesis

1. Quiet staged hero entrance with stable layout and a dominant photograph.
2. Scroll reveals that support the image sequence, plus a single honey-flow study using the supplied animation layers and native shapes.
3. A manual breakfast/tea/serving scene selector with accessible pressed states, stable sizing and no forced autoplay. Shared full-artwork viewer remains available.

Respect global motion preferences, reduced motion and responsive layouts. No new runtime dependency.

## Inspiration applied

The recovered local Behance source audit records 19 examined food/packaging screenshots: JAMMY's label consistency, KAMAKURA's preparation detail, Arôme's connection between pack and use, and Tigris's controlled material photography. The new artwork uses original brand assets and original compositions rather than reference-brand layouts or copy.
