# LizCraft and Pure Hive Honey delivery

## Work completed

LizCraft now includes eight new modern image scenes: expressive colourful fashion, bags and charms, menswear, a mother with school-aged children, homeware, making and two matching parcel states. Four existing artwork compositions and the project cover were refreshed. The source identity, contour and original maker/care wording remain.

Pure Hive Honey has a complete dedicated case study, five new realistic scenes and ten original artwork compositions. It includes identity/palette, label-face and sleeve concepts, lid seals, a gift note, campaign posters, table-card and digital-story artwork. The original mark and wordmark paths were recovered and cleaned, without redrawing.

The broader art direction is contemporary, colourful and human, with African, white and Asian fictional models. Modern stone, glass, steel and bright interiors replace rustic settings. Product concepts are distinguished from existing brand inventory and generated people are not presented as real customers.

## Practical limits

Generated photographs and physical mockups are AI-assisted portfolio visualisations. Exact mark geometry is authoritative in the native SVG artwork; raster mockups are visual approximations. Label artwork needs real product information and production/legal review before manufacture. Browser QA is bounded to the specified layouts and controls; it is not a hardware-device or laboratory performance certification.
