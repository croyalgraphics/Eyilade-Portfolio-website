# Feasts & Flavours: art direction

## Visual thesis

A professional catering identity for lively celebrations and considered service: vivid original red, a playful ornamental signature, colourful food and natural human connection in contemporary event venues, with a secondary intimate-gatherings story at home.

## Content plan

An event-led photographic brand opening; concise source-to-concept context; the original logo, type and palette; professional event service and contemporary shared-meal scenes; three food families already represented in the supplied flyers; practical wrap, seal, carrier, table-card and menu-face artwork; realistic tabletop/takeaway concepts; three campaign compositions; a digital story; original source comparison; a clear close.

Retain the exact mark and supplied wordmark. Use source red #E0012E and charcoal #333333, with white and a soft supporting pink derived from the existing layouts. Use licensed Fredoka for new expressive display type and legible supporting copy. Keep Funky Bird as the supplied outlined wordmark unless redistribution rights are established.

Models are fictional African, white and Asian adults, varied in age and gender, naturally engaged with one another and the food. Scenes use stone, glass, contemporary furniture, clean architecture and realistic daylight. Avoid rustic wood, farmhouse styling, artificial CGI faces and interchangeable stock poses.

The curry, chicken-and-rice and crispy-chicken families come from supplied visual work. New dishes remain visual concepts; do not invent prices, allergies, health/delivery claims, unconfirmed operational details, addresses, testimonials or current ordering contacts. Historic phone/QR data stays out of new collateral. Treat proposed packaging and table applications as portfolio concepts.

## Interaction thesis

1. A concise staged brand-and-photograph entrance.
2. A manual three-occasion editorial sequence (owambes, weddings, birthdays) with clear previous/next controls and stable composition; optional playback with explicit pause and automatic suspension offscreen, hidden, motion-off, focus, hover and an open artwork viewer.
3. A gentle scroll arrival for oversized motif and image spreads, with precise hover/focus affordance for the shared artwork viewer.

Keep interactions different in purpose from Pure Hive's mark-reveal exercise. No new runtime dependency or heavy 3D scene. All supplied work and unrelated pages remain intact.

## References

Apply the previously supplied local Behance study: BRUMO's convivial presentation and small service details, Arôme's practical print/food connection and CluckRiot's coordinated pack/campaign family. Do not copy reference logos, layouts, illustrations or slogans.

## User-confirmed service context

The user clarified during development: Feasts & Flavours is a professional catering service for parties and events, including owambes, weddings, birthdays and family occasions. The main presentation must be event-led. All five previously generated food/family images are retained and delivered; the family scenes support an intimate-gatherings section. New event scenes feature fictional staff and guests, not actual team/client/venue documentation.
