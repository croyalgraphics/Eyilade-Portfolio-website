# LizCraft: colour, people and contemporary life

30 October 2026. Expansion of the completed original identity study.

The exact logo, blue/gold identity, original contour and maker note remain. Product concepts now explore colours beyond the identity palette and a wider range of uses. The five supplied crochet-garment care instructions stay verbatim; the page distinguishes them from care guidance a wider collection would require.

## Image prompt set

All selected imagery was created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. Full-resolution PNGs are retained in artwork/lizcraft/generated, with responsive WebPs in dist/assets/lizcraft. New scenes are AI-assisted collection concepts with fictional models, not claims of existing stock, staff or customers.

Shared prompt constraints: photographic realism, natural expressions and anatomy, convincing yarn construction, no rustic/weathered-wood/farmhouse aesthetic, no new logos/text/watermarks, no CGI. Source Crochet Ideas files were read as product-range inspiration; they are not republished or represented as existing LizCraft products. The first rustic scene proposals were not integrated.

The making/wearing campaigns, digital story and project cover now use the new contemporary photographs. The full original source and previous delivery remain available locally.
