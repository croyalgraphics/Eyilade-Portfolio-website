About Eyilade

A curious mind.
A considered touch.

I’m Eyilade Oridamisi, a senior graphic designer who enjoys joining the dots between a good-looking idea and a useful one.

My path runs through Computer Science, freelance and agency work, and visual design at PwC Nigeria. The most interesting work often starts with a thoughtful question.

Explore my experience

Always asking.
Always making.

I notice everyday friction, question familiar approaches, and explore how things could work differently. Sometimes that means a clearer identity. Sometimes it means a better interaction—or a working prototype.

AI-assisted prototyping is part of that process: a way to test possibilities, learn by making and bring ideas closer to reality.

01 Notice the friction

I look for the small things people work around, and ask what a clearer or more useful experience could be.

02 Make the possibility tangible

I sketch, build and prototype to understand an idea. Making is how I test questions that words alone cannot answer.

03 Keep the human in view

I refine the details around the person using them, connecting a considered visual language with a practical purpose.
